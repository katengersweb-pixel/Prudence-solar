Product Requirements Document (PRD)
1. Product overview
Product name: Prudence Solar & Scientific Solutions Website

Product type: Corporate website with interactive solar estimation and customer enquiry features.

Primary objective: Develop a professional, modern, user-friendly website that communicates Prudence's solar energy solutions, establishes trust, educates potential customers, and generates enquiries for solar products and installations.

The website should retain the company's established identity while improving the presentation, usability, and accessibility of its online presence.
2. Business background
Prudence Solar & Scientific Solutions offers solar-related products and solutions. Its existing website showcases the following product categories:
- Quality solar panels
- Solar street lights
- On-grid solar systems
- Solar motors
- Solar water heaters
- Solar security cameras
The company emphasizes high-quality products, expert installation, reliable after-sales support, performance, and long-term savings.

The new website should communicate these offerings clearly and guide visitors toward contacting the company or requesting a quotation.

7.5 Solar calculator
Purpose: Let customers explore approximate solar system requirements and financial benefits.

This is one of the key interactive features of the website.

Inputs
- Average monthly electricity bill in ₹, or monthly electricity consumption in kWh.
- Optional electricity tariff, if the calculator uses consumption.
- Optional location for more accurate solar-generation estimates.
Outputs
- Estimated solar system capacity in kW.
- Indicative installation cost.
- Estimated monthly or annual electricity bill savings.
- Approximate payback period.
Calculation requirements
1. Validate inputs and explain the assumptions used.
2. Estimate system capacity using an editable, documented calculation method.
3. Calculate approximate installation cost using configurable cost-per-kW assumptions.
4. Estimate savings using configurable generation, tariff, and system-efficiency assumptions.
5. Calculate payback only when the required cost and savings inputs are valid.
6. Provide a disclaimer that actual results depend on roof conditions, shading, location, tariffs, equipment, installation costs, and applicable scheme eligibility.
A basic consumption-based estimate can use:
\[
\text{Estimated capacity (kW)}
=
\frac{\text{Monthly consumption (kWh)}}
{\text{Estimated monthly generation per kW}}
\]

The monthly generation factor must be configurable and appropriate for the selected location. If the calculator uses the electricity bill instead, it must clearly state the tariff assumptions used.

The calculator should not promise guaranteed savings or subsidy amounts. Actual prices and calculation parameters must be confirmed with the company.

User interaction: A Calculate My Solar Savings button updates the results without requiring a page reload. A Request a Quote button should take the customer to the enquiry form, with calculator results optionally carried into the enquiry.

10. Non-functional requirements
Performance
- Optimize image size and loading.
- Avoid excessive animation libraries and unnecessary scripts.
- Minimize layout shifts.
- Keep the initial hero section responsive and fast to load.
Accessibility
- Use semantic HTML and logical heading order.
- Provide alternative text for meaningful images.
- Support keyboard navigation.
- Maintain sufficient text contrast.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
Security and privacy
- Validate all submitted form data on the server where applicable.
- Never expose API keys or secrets in frontend code.
- Collect only the personal information needed for enquiries.
- Provide a clear privacy notice.
- Use HTTPS in production.
SEO
- Add a descriptive page title and meta description.
- Use meaningful headings and image alt text.
- Include the company name and relevant solar services naturally.
- Configure canonical URLs and sitemap when the domain is finalized.
- Add structured business data only with verified company information.

13. Development phases in Antigravity
To avoid overwhelming Antigravity with all requirements at once, implementation should follow this sequence.
1. Project inspection and setup: Inspect the repository, identify the framework, establish the design system, and confirm the responsive page structure.
2. Homepage and branding: Implement the header, four-image hero carousel, trusted partner section, company statistics, and main navigation.
3. Products and content sections: Add the six product categories, PM Surya Ghar section, customer gallery, reviews, and footer.
4. Interactive features: Implement and test the solar calculator and enquiry form, including the necessary submission integration.
5. Polish and validation: Improve animations, responsive behavior, accessibility, performance, SEO, and error handling.
6. Client handover: Confirm real company information, test the production deployment, and document any remaining configuration requirements.
Each phase should be tested before moving to the next. Antigravity should make targeted edits rather than replacing the entire project unnecessarily.

15. Scope boundaries
The first release includes the corporate homepage, product showcase, rotating hero images, company statistics, PM Surya Ghar information, solar calculator, customer gallery, reviews, enquiry form, and contact section.

The following are not automatically included unless the client requests them:
- E-commerce checkout and online payments.
- Customer accounts or dashboards.
- Inventory management.
- Complex solar engineering simulations.
- Government subsidy application processing.
- A full content-management system.
- Advanced analytics or CRM integration beyond the agreed enquiry workflow.
This keeps the project focused on the client's requirements while leaving room for future expansion.

Final product definition
Prudence Solar & Scientific Solutions will have a responsive, professional corporate website that showcases its solar products, builds customer trust, helps visitors estimate potential solar benefits, and makes it easy to enquire about products and installation services.

The logo, brand colors, existing product categories, and nine requested homepage sections form the foundation. The calculator and enquiry workflow provide the primary interactive functionality
